# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in Obsidian Journal is expected to help maintain a
respectful, welcoming environment, regardless of background, identity, or level
of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give constructive feedback about the work and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and differing perspectives.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help resolve their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or helps investigate one.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces, as well as public activity where someone represents the project.
It applies to contributors and maintainers alike.

## Reporting concerns

The repository owner, [@TheMakunga](https://github.com/themakunga), is
responsible for enforcement. Use a private contact method listed on the owner's
GitHub profile. If none is available, open an issue requesting a private contact
channel without including incident details or personal information.

Reports should describe what happened and include relevant context when it is
safe to share. Maintainers should limit access to report details to those needed
to investigate and respond, while respecting the privacy of everyone involved.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and request changes in behavior.
Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a warning, a
temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces. Serious incidents
may result in immediate removal. Decisions should be proportionate, explained
when safe, and applied consistently.
